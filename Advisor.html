Educational only; not investment advice; not from a SEBI-registered adviser. Historical CAGR/vol/drawdown are 20.2-yr backtests (2006-2026). Pledging leverages a portfolio that itself draws down — deploy ≤60% of usable margin and keep a buffer. Haircuts and the 50:50 cash:non-cash rule are indicative and broker-dependent.
